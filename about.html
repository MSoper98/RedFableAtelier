About Us!

Red

A 27 year old with a love for animals, fantasy, books, gaming, and being at home. With several years in the craft circle, she has done various types such as polymer clay, resin, ribbon flower making, scrapbooking, sewing, and so on! Red is your resident artisan, handling detail work with an impatient but steady hand. Often called a choas gremlin, she's been left in charge of packing orders, remembering to order supplies, crafting all the goods, and being the hectice social media manager.
