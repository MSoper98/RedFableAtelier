About Red Fable!

A 28 year old with a love for animals, fantasy, books, gaming, and being at home. With several years in the craft circle, I have done various types such as polymer clay, resin, ribbon flower making, sewing, and so on!
As your resident artisan, I handle all the detail work with an impatient but steady hand. Often called a chaos gremlin, I've been left in charge of packing orders, remembering to order supplies, crafting all the goods, and being the hectic social media manager.
I'm your atelier owner, "Red"!
