Ask the Tomes of Wisdom

How long until my wares are ready?

Shipping
All our wares are handmade by our in-house artisan. Depending on the order quantity & item sizes, the artisan can take from 2-7 business days. Our owls deliver on a standard basis of 3-7 business days, depending on your location.

Do you complete custom requests?

Customs
We do indeed take custom requests! You can send us your idea through carrier owl on our Custom Order page, though we make no guarantee that the artisan will fulfill it.

What about returning wares?

Returns
Unfortunately, on custom requests we are unable to accept returns. However, on specific circumstances we are willing to do an equivalent exchange if an item arrives severely damaged. If that is an issue, send us a letter through the Contact section and we will be happy to see what can be done.
